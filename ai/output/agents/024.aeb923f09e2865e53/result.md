## Resolution

**No sort-by-item-count step exists anywhere in the Humble Games-bundle path.** The apparent contradiction is fully explained: the real on-disk `tier-1.yml`/`tier-2.yml`/`tier-3.yml` for `2026-06-17_upload-vr-summer-2026` were **not** produced by today's `crawler.py`. That heuristic never consults `archive.tiers`/`tier_order` at all for this filename shape, so it's a different, independent ordering source from the crawler's live output.

**Verified against the real captured source** (`archives/humblebundle/bundle/2026-06-17_upload-vr-summer-2026/source.json`): `bundle_data.tier_order == ["bt18", "bt15", "initial"]` with item counts 9, 5, 2 — i.e. Humble's own `tier_order` is genuinely descending, full-tier-first, matching the parser fixture (`tests/test_humblebundle_parser.py:87` → `archive.tiers[0].name == "Entire 2 Item Bundle"`, asserted directly at line 134). `metadata.json` for that same offer confirms `archive.tiers == [bt18(9), bt15(5), initial(2)]` — still descending, unchanged by any sort.
